## 5. RISKS/OPEN

1. **RESOLVED (was BLOCKING) -- flight-recorder aliasing.** `bus.record`/`replay` (`EventBus.js:333`, `:415`) store payloads by reference, so a reused per-tag scratch would make replay redraw the last frame N times. SETTLED in S1 task 5 (owner, 2026-08-30): the live `bus` is NOT recorded; a dedicated numeric `traceBus` records `mid` by value for replay. **S2 therefore reuses per-tag scratch (`D`/`Q`/`T`) on `bus` freely** and adds nothing to the recorder -- the handlers consume synchronously inside the emit. Coder inherits `traceBus` from S1; on each accepted DEPTH/QUOTE frame also `traceBus.emit('mid', f.mid)`. Rejected alternatives (recorded for the S7 ledger): monomorphic scratch (orthogonal to the reference-lifetime bug), demo-owned SoA/pool ring (honest + zero-GC but drops the bus flight-recorder showcase).
2. **Price-trace density.** `@depth20@100ms` yields 10 price points/s where `@bookTicker` yielded hundreds -- the tape polyline will visibly starve. Options: keep `btcusdt@bookTicker` as a **third** combined stream (contradicts the roadmap's two-stream URL but preserves the trace and the `QUOTE` tag on a live source), or drive the tape from depth mid at 10 Hz. Recommend three streams; needs your call.
3. **Ladder bar scale.** The hardcoded `ladderW / 48` divisor (kernel.js:225-226, :288) was tuned for synthesised sizes 4-44. Real BTC depth qty spans ~0.001-50. Needs a running-max normaliser -- decide whether it is per-frame max (jittery) or an EMA-smoothed max (extra state, still zero-alloc).
4. `depth20` is a **partial-book snapshot**, not a diff stream: no `U`/`u` sequence maintenance, no resync logic. Confirm that is intended (it is what makes the zero-alloc apply possible).
5. No `package.json` in `market-map/` and no `test/` dir yet. Confirm the tests run from the `LiteDiContainer` package's script surface (its devDeps carry `lite-leak` / `lite-gc-profiler`) versus giving the demo its own -- assertion 3/4 use plain `--expose-gc` + `process.memoryUsage()` so they do not require the gate packages either way.
